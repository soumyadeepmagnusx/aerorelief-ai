1. Executive Summary & The Track 5 Problem Shift

In tropical cyclone hotspots—most critically the Bay of Bengal and coastal APAC—disaster management has historically been reactive, fragmented, and late. The operational protocol has relied on post-landfall recovery: waiting until storm winds recede before dispatching optical survey aircraft, waiting 45 to 90 days for bureaucratic loss assessments, and routing relief convoys on generic consumer navigation maps that lead to fatal vehicle drownings.

The Core Paradigm Shift: Anticipatory Action

AeroRelief AI fundamentally changes the disaster timeline from post-landfall damage recovery to pre-landfall anticipatory defense. By marrying Google Gemini 3.7 Flash multimodal reasoning with Google Earth Engine (GEE) cloud-penetrating synthetic aperture radar (SAR), hydrodynamic riverbed modeling, and decentralized parametric smart contracts, critical interventions are automated 4 to 12 hours before the storm eyewall makes landfall.
